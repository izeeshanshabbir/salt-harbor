Salt Harbor

A small harbor city at sunset, a few jobs on the board, and patrol cars that notice everything. Walk or drive into an amber marker to take a job.

  • W A S D move, or gas and steer
  • Mouse look around
  • Shift sprint on foot
  • E get in or out of a car
  • Click or F fire on foot
  • Space handbrake in a car
  • H horn L headlights
  • R radio M mute P pause
  • T toggle rain
An original game prototype. Progress saves in this browser.
