Salt Harbor

A small harbor city at sunset, a few jobs on the board, and patrol cars that notice everything. Walk into an amber marker to take a job.

  • W A S D move, or gas and steer
  • Mouse look around
  • Shift sprint on foot
  • E get in or out of a car
  • Click or F fire on foot
  • Space handbrake in a car
  • M mute P pause
An original game prototype. Cars, pedestrians and police all share one city, so how you drive matters.
